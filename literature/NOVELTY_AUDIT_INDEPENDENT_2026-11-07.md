# Independent adversarial novelty audit: charged scalar quirkonium WZ signed interference
**Date:** 2026-11-07. **Verdict:** conditional model-specific novelty, not a publishable standalone claim yet.
**Scope:** no detector simulation; effective LO SU(2) doublet scalar 0+ quirkonium, on-shell WZ and Wgamma. Complete published-paper absence is not certified.

## Exact claim tested
M=500 GeV, tanbeta=3 and tanbeta=0.394724021524, with
Delta=mW^2 cos(2beta)/M (different constituent masses),
have identical f00(WZ)=0.909281448 and normalized BR(WZ)=0.753081645
*when WZ/Wgamma are the only charged ground-state channels*.
They have opposite a1, approximately -0.12030511 vs +0.12030511, in
dP/dPhi=[1+a1 cos(Phi)+a2 cos(2Phi)]/(2pi).
The model-specific mathematical effect is internally PASS at 7 masses
250..2000 GeV and spin-correlated independent MG5 2->4
checks reproduce a1 = ±0.12041507 on a finite angle grid.
The analytic closed value ±0.12030511 differs from finite quadrature;
this is a known numerical quadrature rather than a phase disagreement.
These statements do NOT establish realistic model viability or reach.

## What cannot be called novel

- WZ/Wgamma squirkonium LO widths and branching ratios.
- Helicity decomposition or longitudinal fraction f00 alone.
- Joint W/Z decay-plane cosPhi and cos2Phi harmonics in spin-zero
  diboson decays; general helicity-relative-phase discrimination.
- "Rates plus helicity fractions can conceal phases" as a general theorem.
- Independent MG5 validation, by itself, as a physics discovery.

## What remains a plausible *incremental model-specific* result

For Forsyth's particular charged scalar doublet with D-term splitting,
there appear to be two **normalised-channel-rate-degenerate** choices
of tanbeta, M fixed, which give opposite *relative* WZ helicity signs.
None of the checked direct quirkonium texts explicitly identifies that
particular two-branch construction or a trilepton sign test.
But this is a restricted model realization of familiar phase tomography;
a publishable paper requires extra physical content, not just the formula.

## Model-domain caveat (STOP until checked)

For25 Sec 3 at Eq (3.2) calls tanbeta the SUSY Higgs VEV ratio and
says tanbeta>1, whereas its 2026 erratum explicitly scans
0.1<=tanbeta<=10 as the relevant splitting interval. It is incorrect
to automatically declare tanbeta<1 excluded or physically validated
from these statements. The low branch tanbeta~0.395 may be mathematically
permitted in the simplified D-term parametrization but requires a
consistent scalar/Higgs sector and UV running/perturbativity argument.
In a type-II-like two-doublet convention, sin(beta)~0.367 and the
tree-level top Yukawa is about 2.7; this is large and potentially
runs rapidly, but **not proof of a pole below the weak scale**.
Bound-state beta-decay versus cooling and absolutely normalized rates
also differ between the two Delta solutions; BR equality alone
does not guarantee equal production counts.

## Observability caveat (STOP before collider reach claims)

For W->e/mu nu and Z->ee/mumu, daughter BRs are approximately
0.216 and 0.0673, respectively. With BR(Psi->WZ)=0.7531,
only ~0.01095 of produced charged bound states enter prompt
e/mu trileptons before acceptance, reconstruction and competing processes.
At L=3000 fb^-1, even the ideal moment-based 3sigma sign-discrimination
target of ~313 fully selected signal events needs an effective bound-state
production cross section >=9.5 fb if *everything* passes acceptance;
5sigma ~868 events needs >=26.4 fb at 100% efficiency. A true measurable
rate needs larger values, with an efficiency penalty. These numbers are
simple necessary signal-yield diagnostics for the chosen moment estimator,
not ATLAS sensitivity projections, universal likelihood limits, nor
estimates of the actual model production cross section.
Gro24 Table 7 independently shows large neutrino-reconstruction
distortions to WZ angular coefficients; fiducial acceptance also
breaks naive inclusive moment projections.
