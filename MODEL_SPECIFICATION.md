# Preliminary physical model gate — NOT FROZEN

## Candidate 1: vectorlike fermionic quirk doublet

New confining gauge group SU(N_IC), with N_IC >= 2 and a massive Dirac doublet Q=(U,D)^T in the fundamental of SU(N_IC), electroweak representation (2,Y=1/2), SM color singlet. Vectorlike left and right components have the same gauge representations, making the gauge anomalies cancel within the Dirac pair. Charges Q_U=+1 and Q_D=0.

Representative renormalizable terms:
```text
L = -1/4 G_IC^{a mu nu} G^a_{IC mu nu}
    + Qbar (i gamma^mu D_mu - M_Q) Q
```
The vectorlike mass is gauge invariant before electroweak symmetry breaking.

Candidate charged composite is U Dbar (Q=+1), with ^1S_0 and ^3S_1 investigated separately. **The physical amplitude and rate are not yet checked to be nonzero for these precise quantum numbers.** No C parity is assigned to a charged bound state.

## Candidate 2: scalar bound state

Use the explicit scalar SU(3)_IC doublet model in Forsyth et al. (2025), §3 and App. A.3, **with its April 2026 erratum**. Avoid transferring fermionic wavefunction/spin projectors to scalar constituents. The corrected restricted mass splitting in the 2026 erratum is Δ = m_W² cos(2β)/M = (m_W²/M)(1−tan²β)/(1+tan²β), with the erratum displaying 0.1 ≤ tanβ ≤ 10. This relation applies to the paper's stated approximation (lifted singlets and neglected off-diagonal mixing), not arbitrary scalar models. The scalar masses and splitting must arise from the actual scalar mass matrix and be compatible with the erratum; a free splitting is merely exploratory until a complete scalar potential and electroweak constraints are checked.

## Universal facts
- Charged spin-zero → W γ (both on shell): W longitudinal helicity amplitude forbidden by angular momentum/helicity selection rules. If Wγ width vanishes due to charges/couplings, no W polarization fraction exists in that channel.
- Charged spin-zero → W Z: longitudinal-longitudinal helicity is kinematically allowed, but its *magnitude* requires the complete model amplitude.
- An unpolarized spin-zero parent emits W/Z isotropically. The daughter **lepton polar angles** retain polarization information.
- Any scalar/fermion comparison must control the dependence on unknown bound-state formation and spin-population fractions.
