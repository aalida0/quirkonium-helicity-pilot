# Helicity and lepton-angle definitions

Parent is at rest; W+ moves along +z and Z or γ along −z. Use metric (+---); polarizations satisfy \(e_\lambda\cdot k=0\), \(e_\lambda^*\cdot e_{\lambda'}=-\delta_{\lambda\lambda'}\). Spin-zero selection requires \(\lambda_W=\lambda_V\) in the Jacob–Wick convention. On-shell Wγ longitudinal polarization is forbidden.

Define \(\theta_\ell\) as the W+ charged-lepton polar angle in the W rest frame relative to the W momentum direction in the quirkonium rest frame. For the physical parity-even scalar threshold amplitude there is an equal transverse ± helicity mixture, and the ideal azimuth-integrated W decay distribution is
\[
\frac{1}{\Gamma}\frac{d\Gamma}{d\cos\theta_\ell}
=\frac34 f_{00}(1-\cos^2\theta_\ell)+\frac38(1-f_{00})(1+\cos^2\theta_\ell).
\]
The individual transverse W helicity densities have parity-odd linear terms that cancel only in the equal mixture. No acceptance, cuts, detector response, neutrino reconstruction, off-shell effects or production polarization modeled. The marginal W-only plot is supplemented by the full ideal **joint three-angle** W/Z decay-density and azimuth harmonics in theory/joint_angular_interference.md; these include the charged-lepton Z chiral couplings and longitudinal–transverse interference. Neither calculation includes detector cuts or off-shell effects.
