# Pilot decision — provisional conditional NO-GO (2026-11-07)

**Question:** Do normalized helicity and charged-lepton angular distributions of charged quirkonium → Wγ/WZ distinguish consistent physical quirk models beyond published integrated widths?

**Established:** S-wave charged quirkonium integrated Wγ/WZ widths are old. Spin-zero Wγ polarization is entirely transverse, hence not a useful new discriminant. A scalar WZ mode may support longitudinal polarization; its model-dependent fraction has not been demonstrated with a validated tensor calculation.

**Literature status:** relevant primary sources identified: Fok & Kribs 2011, Cheung/Keung/Yuan 2008/09, Kahawala/Kats 2011, Forsyth et al. 2025 plus 2026 erratum. INSPIRE citation graph not exhaustively inspected. See literature/ directory.

**Benchmark:** vectorlike fermion SU(N_IC) doublet with Y=+1/2, plus a prospective scalar comparison with the corrected Forsyth model. No benchmark has yet passed the complete gauge/rate validation.

**Physics blockers:** independent WZ helicity tensor and full-diagram Ward checks; helicity-summed normalized rates matching publication; actual scalar mass matrix/mass-splitting constraints; spin-formation dependence; full cited/citing literature closure.

**Decision:** Conditional NO-GO for expanding to paper or scan. GO only for a short *calculation pilot* tackling one scalar WZ benchmark, which should terminate if novel normalized information disappears or a direct prior calculation is discovered. Do not run detector simulations.

**Next minimal calculation:** set up the published scalar field content and masses, contract the **complete** four-point/seagull and exchange diagrams with explicit W/Z polarization vectors, check both longitudinal and transverse results, compare summed widths to Forsyth Eq. (40), and independently cross-check with scalar-QED electroweak Feynman rules. Only then produce W/Z decay-angle plots.
