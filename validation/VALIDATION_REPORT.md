# Validation report — 2026-11-07 scalar threshold pilot

## Claim and evidence standard

Computed the **complete physical LO threshold** \(\widetilde u\,\widetilde d^*\to W^+Z,W^+\gamma\) amplitude from a gauge-coupled scalar doublet and compared the helicity-summed decay width with Forsyth et al., *Visible collider signals of natural quirks*, arXiv:2504.02940v2, Appendix A.3 Eqs. (A.4)-(A.8). Used the April 2026 mass-splitting erratum (PRD 113 079901).

Input fixed on-shell EW scheme: \(m_W=80.379\) GeV, \(m_Z=91.1876\) GeV, \(\alpha_W=0.0338\), \(Q_u=2/3\), \(Q_d=-1/3\). Bound state: a **fixed scalar spin-zero state**, \(M=m_u+m_d\), \(\Delta=m_W^2\cos2\beta/M\). Unknown common wavefunction factor is set to \(|R(0)|^2=1\) GeV³ for rate comparison only.

## Results

Tests: 9 Python unittest cases **PASS in local Python 3.13.5**, including 70 points for source-width reproduction (7 masses × 5 tanβ values × 2 channels), 20 Wγ photon Ward configurations (5 masses × 4 tanβ values), scalar selection rule, fixed-state wavefunction cancellation, zero-splitting exact limit, high-mass asymptotic fraction and ideal lepton-angle normalization. Exact source code and checks: \`src/scalar_threshold_amplitudes.py\`, \`tests/test_scalar_threshold.py\`.

- Numeric independent diagram-amplitude/source-width ratio: WZ residual max O(10^-13) in explored grid; Wγ O(10^-15), consistent with floating-point algebra. These are **algebraic reference matches**, not realistic systematic uncertainties.
- Photon Ward test: replacing photon polarization with its momentum for both transverse and longitudinal W polarizations gives max normalized residual O(10^-15) within scanned points. Both exchange, seagull and s-channel are included.
- \(f_{00}(WZ)\) physical helicity contraction equals the longitudinal piece of the published squared-width expression, with a clean degenerate-mass and asymptotic-limit cross-check.
- \(W\gamma\) longitudinal \(W\) vanishes identically in the spinless two-body channel.
- \(|R(0)|^2\) cancels from the fixed-state normalized fractions at leading nonrelativistic factorization order.

### Addendum: joint W/Z leptonic angles (same date)

Five additional local tests have passed (total **14**) for the ideal on-shell narrow-width spin-one decay density including Z left/right lepton couplings and W+ helicity. The derived decay-plane density is \(dP/d\Phi=[1+a_1\cos\Phi+a_2\cos2\Phi]/(2\pi)\). Analytic coefficients and derivation appear in theory/joint_angular_interference.md. Numerical polar-angle+azimuth integration at M=500 GeV, tanβ=1 and 3, confirms normalization to **within 7e-4** and both moments to within **5e-4** on finite midpoint quadrature. Angular density is nonnegative on a test grid and \(a_2=(1-f_{00})/4\). The **relative longitudinal–transverse interference phase** enters \(a_1\).

**Gate:** joint ideal lepton angular correlations = PASS scoped (narrow-width on-shell and no cuts). **Not closed:** independent generator, full scalar potential viability, actual reconstructed observability, full cited/citing novelty audit. The initial nine-test statement above is the original threshold subset, not the updated total.
